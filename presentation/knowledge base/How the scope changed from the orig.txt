How the scope changed from the original Yacoub/Obid direction
-
(wide scope)
* the orignal implementaition was quite broad , especially in full n8n deployment on raspberry Pi
	* docker/container optimization 
	* middleware 
	* agent workflow 
	* safety evalution
	* broader Obid side agentic logic 
* The broader assignment could be interpreted as pushing agentic AI in n8n quite far, including multi-agent workflows, different devices, different models, and deeper performance/scalability analysis. 
-
(narrow scope)
* to make the thesis finishable, I narrowed it into one contrabble IoT scenario
	* I kept the core parts of the original idea 	
		* self hosted n8n 
		* python middleware
		* API endpoints
		* workflow logic 
		* contract safety boundary
		* performance/resource management 
		* raspberry pi validation
	* The purpose was to avoid spreading the work across too many partially finished ideas. Instead, 	I focused on one path that could actually be implemented, tested, measured, and discussed 	properly in the report.  
-
* (5. Why I chose middleware on Raspberry Pi instead of full n8n on Raspberry Pi)
	* middleware on Pi
		* By moving only the middleware Pi, I could test whether the workflow can trigger 			actions on a constrained device (Pi) over the network and collect latency, CPU/RAM, and 		thermal evidence.
			* So the Pi part strengthens the edge-computing angle without turning 					the whole project into a full Raspberry Pi deployment project.
			* also proves * n8n can stay as the brain on the PC
	* n8n workflow/docker stable on the PC. 
		* Running the full n8n/Docker/AI stack on the Pi would have changed the project into a 			much larger deployment optimization task. That is useful future work, but for this 			thesis I wanted a controlled Tier 1.5 validation rather than a risky full redesign.
