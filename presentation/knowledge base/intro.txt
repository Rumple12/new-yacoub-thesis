Delegation of Intelligence (The Brain): Heavy lifting, API calls, and LLM reasoning stay on the powerful PC inside n8n.
-
Delegation of Safety & Execution (The Bouncer): The Raspberry Pi doesn't need to be smart; it just needs to be strict. Because the middleware enforces the JSON contracts and the safety boundaries, it acts as an absolute firewall.
-
Even if an AI agent hallucinates and tells the system to "spin the crane at 5,000 RPM," the middleware catches it, looks at the safety contract, sees it's blocked, and rejects it
-
That separation of logic and safety is the real engineering feat here. You've built a blueprint for safe autonomous control.
