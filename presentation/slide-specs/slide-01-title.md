# Slide 1 - Title

## Point
Introduce the thesis and frame it correctly: the project is about the workflow-to-action architecture, not just a fan demo.

## Slide text
Design and Evaluation of an n8n-Based Workflow-to-Action Architecture
for IoT-Style Fan Control

PC-hosted n8n, Python middleware, and Raspberry Pi middleware validation

Yacoub Dawli
DT099G Bachelor Thesis
Mid Sweden University

## Speaker notes
Hi, my name is Yacoub Dawli, and this is my bachelor thesis in DT099G.

My thesis is about building and evaluating a small IoT-style workflow system. The example I use is a fan that turns on or off depending on temperature.

But the important part is not the fan itself. The important part is the structure around it: how a workflow makes a decision, how that decision becomes an action, and how the result can be tested and measured.

## Possible opponent question
Isn’t this basically just a simple fan control demo?

## Answer
The fan control is intentionally simple, because it is used as a test case. The thesis is not mainly about the fan hardware. It is about the workflow-to-action structure around it: n8n, Python middleware, deterministic and agent-enhanced workflows, contracts, evaluation evidence, and Raspberry Pi middleware validation.
