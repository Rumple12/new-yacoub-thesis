# Slide 15 - Societal contribution

## Point
Show that the fan example is a controlled proxy for a broader event-validation-action pattern. The societal value is responsible, traceable automation: event input, workflow decision, contract/validation boundary, middleware action, and recorded evidence.

## Slide text
Title:
Societal contribution

Main:
The fan is a controlled proxy.
The real value is the event -> validation -> action pattern.

Pattern:
Event -> Workflow decision -> Contract / validation boundary -> Middleware action -> Recorded evidence

Broader relevance:
- Smart buildings: ventilation, heating, lighting
- Industrial monitoring: alerts and controlled responses
- Environmental sensing: air quality, humidity, water level
- Health / environment monitoring: traceable notifications or approved actions

Honesty note:
Possible future use cases, not operational deployment.

Final line:
The exciting part is not the fan.
It is the responsible structure around future event-driven systems.

## Visual
Use a clean horizontal event-validation-action-evidence flow and four broader relevance cards.
Use the city/network image as a faint full-slide background accent, behind all text and cards.

## Presenter guidance
Point to the proxy message when explaining that the fan is only a controlled example.
Point across the pattern when explaining event, decision, validation, action, and evidence.
Point to the cards when explaining possible societal use cases.
Point to the honesty note when saying this is not operational deployment.
Look back at the audience for the final line.

## Possible opponent question
Are you claiming your system is ready for smart buildings or industry?

## Answer
No. I am not claiming operational readiness. The slide shows possible societal relevance of the architecture pattern. Real deployment would require physical sensors, actuator testing, stronger runtime validation, safety work, and broader evaluation.

## Possible opponent question
Why talk about society if your implementation only controls a simulated fan?

## Answer
Because the fan is a controlled proxy. It lets me test the structure safely and clearly. The broader contribution is the event-validation-action pattern: event input, workflow decision, contract-shaped action, validation boundary, middleware execution, and recorded evidence.
